Rolling-WAMOfficial project architecture verified · direct stable image asset not separately verified

Rolling-WAM — World Action Models with Rolling Imagination

Research PaperWorld Models / Physical AIVLA / EmbodiedSep 25

Problem: jointly denoising an entire future video-action horizon at every replanning cycle makes world-action models too slow for responsive control.

Approach: maintains a sliding video-action window at staggered noise levels: the imminent action chunk finishes denoising while farther-future chunks remain partially refined and roll forward after new observations.

Why it matters: the project reports 4.5× faster steady-state replanning, 98.1% LIBERO, 93.3% RoboTwin 2.0 and 85.0% real-world Unitree G1 success.

restart future prediction → continuously rolling imagination

Interfaces are becoming the representation itself

Today’s strongest work makes causal evidence, visual actions, physical language and imagined failures into explicit objects that models can transfer, optimize and act on.

Research & Releases

MCDCausal multimodal ICL distillation

MCD — Causal Distillation of Multimodal In-Context Learning

Research PaperVLM / LVLMSep 30

Problem: small LVLMs can copy teacher outputs while still relying on language priors or spurious prompt cues.

Approach: structure-preserving token interventions identify which multimodal evidence causally supports the teacher’s answer, then distill that evidence-use pattern.

Why it matters: across three LVLM families and seven benchmarks, MCD reports +7.23 points on average and +4.68 over vanilla distillation.

distill answers → distill causal evidence usage

Dream4ACTShared visual action interface across embodiments

Dream4ACT — Shared Visual Action Interface for Multi-Embodiment Modeling

Research PaperVLA / EmbodiedWorld Models / Physical AISep 30

Problem: robot joint vectors differ in dimension and semantics across embodiments and do not align naturally with video-model priors.

Approach: target joint configurations are rendered from four virtual cameras via URDF forward kinematics, turning action into a shared visual representation.

Why it matters: observation and action can share one video autoencoder/diffusion transformer; Dream4ACT reports 88.98% average success on RoboTwin 2.0.

embodiment-specific joint vectors → common visual action language
