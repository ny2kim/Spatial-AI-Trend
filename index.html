FBIS-73M — Planet-Scale Field Boundary Data

Model/Data ReleaseGeospatial AIRecent Hub release

Problem: agricultural parcel records often merge multiple physical fields and vary drastically in imagery resolution.

Approach: FBIS-73M provides about 1.47M image patches and 73M field instances across 61 countries at 0.25–10 m resolution, with resolution-specific remediation of parcel/field mismatches.

Why it matters: Delineate Anything v2 uses the dataset for resolution-agnostic global field delineation and reports a +103.3% relative mAP@0.5 gain over its predecessor, with a separate 100-country evaluation benchmark.

regional parcel labels → curated multi-resolution global field instances

Rolling-WAM — World Action Models with Rolling Imagination

Research PaperWorld Models / Physical AIVLA / EmbodiedSep 25

Problem: jointly denoising an entire future video-action horizon at every replanning cycle makes world-action models too slow for responsive control.

Approach: maintains a sliding video-action window at staggered noise levels: the imminent action chunk finishes denoising while farther-future chunks remain partially refined and roll forward after new observations.

Why it matters: the project reports 4.5× faster steady-state replanning, 98.1% LIBERO, 93.3% RoboTwin 2.0 and 85.0% real-world Unitree G1 success.

restart future prediction → continuously rolling imagination
