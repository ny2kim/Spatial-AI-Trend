Hybrid spatial reasoning: explicit 3D state + frozen MLLMs

Today’s main signal is architectural: 3D spatial intelligence may not need to be learned entirely inside the MLLM. A compact external 3D scene representation can route different spatial questions to the right form of evidence.

Notable paper

GraFT: A Training-Free Framework for Spatial Reasoning in Multimodal Large Language Models via 3D Scene Graphs

arXivSep 3, 20263D Spatial ReasoningTraining-Free

Problem: Current MLLMs remain unreliable at precise metric geometry, egocentric↔allocentric transformation, and fine-grained spatial grounding.

Approach: GraFT keeps the MLLM frozen and uses a compact 3D scene graph as persistent spatial state. Queries are routed to symbolic geometric tools for exact metric questions, bird’s-eye-view rendering for allocentric layout, or geometry-guided egocentric frame retrieval for appearance grounding.

Why it matters: Some spatial failures may be better solved by giving a general MLLM the right structured evidence and tools instead of forcing the backbone to internalize every form of 3D reasoning.

The reported results show substantial gains over frozen MLLM baselines on VSI-Bench and improvements on ScanQA from geometry-guided evidence selection.

Main trend:
frozen MLLM → explicit 3D world state → task-specific spatial evidence → tool-assisted reasoning

Open GraFT on arXiv →

Trend synthesis

Explicit world state is returning.

Scene graphs, map-like representations, symbolic geometry, and viewpoint retrieval can complement pretrained multimodal reasoning instead of being replaced by it.

Hybrid systems are becoming credible.

Exact geometry can be delegated to symbolic tools, while the MLLM handles semantic and appearance reasoning.

What changed?

The previous research thread emphasized action-sufficient learned representations. Today’s direction adds a complementary route: maintain part of the world state explicitly and let the MLLM query it as needed.

pixels / video → 3D scene graph → geometry / BEV / egocentric evidence → frozen MLLM → reasoning / planning

Key takeaway

Likely future architecture:

Learned latent representations for perception/action + explicit spatial state for metric reasoning, memory, verification, and planning.
