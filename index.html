WLA³ — World Latent Action Modeling

Research PaperVLA / EmbodiedWorld ModelsSep 14

Problem: human and robot video lack a common low-noise action label space.

Approach: synchronized world-state transitions become compact latent actions and richer features reused for semantics, dynamics and embodiment-specific controls.

Why it matters: human video becomes scalable action supervision; the paper reports 81.9% average success across six real-robot tasks versus 66.2% for π0.5.

world transition → latent action → semantics + dynamics + control

Research PaperVLM / LVLMSep 15
