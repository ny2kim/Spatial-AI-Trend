Research PaperVLM / Multimodal LLMSep 18 · surfaced Sep 21

Problem: audio-video assistants often depend on separate text questions, ASR, or captions; native dialogue recordings are scarce and hard to evaluate.

FOCAL-VLA — Subtask-Guided Geometry + Implicit World Modeling

Research PaperVLA / EmbodiedWorld ModelsSep 18 · updated Sep 21

Problem: scene-wide geometry and future prediction can overwhelm a VLA with irrelevant structure during precise manipulation.

ForceTwinOfficial project verified · safe fallback

ForceTwin — Physics-Informed Digital Twins from Human Interaction

Research PaperWorld Models / Physical AIRoboticsSep 18 · updated Sep 21

Problem: appearance and kinematics do not reveal inertia, friction, damping, springs, or state-dependent mechanism forces.

Approach: humans probe articulated objects with a force-sensing gripper; synchronized pose and force identify articulation, parametric dynamics, and a neural residual.

Why it matters: ForceTwin nearly halves inertial-parameter error versus a VLM prior and reports 87% goal completion across nine object/embodiment pairs versus 60% with VLM-prior twins.

visual twin → measured interaction forces → control-ready physical twin

AtomEgo — Ego-Robot Integration for Embodied Pretraining

Research PaperVLA / EmbodiedWorld-Action Models

Problem: human egocentric video is abundant but differs sharply from robot embodiment and action spaces.

Approach: studies three co-training paradigms over about 2,659 hours: domain-specific action heads, progressive embodiment alignment, and joint video-action modeling.
