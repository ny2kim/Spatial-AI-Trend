Friday · September 11, 2026

Interfaces, cross-view dynamics, model selection, and explicit world state

Today’s strongest signal is that capability alone is not enough: recent work is making the interface between reasoning and action, views and dynamics, models and tasks, and world state and rendering much more explicit.

Research

Show-Harness

Show-Harness — Just a VLM Agent Can Play Robots

Research PaperVLM/LVLMVLA / EmbodiedarXiv · Sep 10

Problem: foundation VLMs understand scenes well, but turning that intelligence into fine-grained robot control usually requires a separate policy stack.

Approach: Show-Harness exposes compact semantic action units to the VLM and uses embodiment-specific interpreters to deterministically ground each unit into local robot motion.

Why it matters: the same interface supports zero-shot frontier VLM control and low-cost fine-tuning of small open VLMs across multiple robot embodiments.

general VLM → semantic action interface → robot execution

DUET-DINO
Cross-view latent planning

DUET-DINO — Simultaneous Cross-View World Modeling

Research PaperWorld ModelsVLA / EmbodiedarXiv · Sep 10

Problem: single-view latent world models struggle with fine-grained spatial and rotational control for full 7-DoF manipulation.

Approach: DUET-DINO jointly predicts action-conditioned side- and wrist-camera latent futures with cross-view conditioning, combining global scene context with gripper-centric detail.

Why it matters: multi-view world modeling directly improves latent planning, reaching reported 92% reach, 72.5% angled-reach, and 60% lift success.

side view + wrist view → shared latent dynamics → 7-DoF planning

REMSA

REMSA — Foundation Model Selection for Remote Sensing

ECCV 2026Geospatial AIAgent

Problem: choosing among the rapidly growing set of remote-sensing foundation models is difficult because capabilities, modalities and deployment constraints are scattered across heterogeneous documentation.

Approach: REMSA combines a structured database of 150+ remote-sensing foundation models with query interpretation, metadata-grounded retrieval, LLM ranking and clarification loops.

Why it matters: the geospatial problem is shifting from “build one FM” toward agentic orchestration and task-specific model selection.

task constraints → structured RSFM retrieval → agentic model selection

Programmable World Model

Programmable World Model

Research PaperWorld ModelsarXiv · Sep 10

Problem: interactive video world models can look realistic while losing persistent entity state, off-screen information and explicit game rules.

Approach: an agent converts natural-language rules into executable programs that update a persistent global state, then state-augmented 3D boxes condition a pretrained video renderer.

Why it matters: it explicitly separates world logic from visual rendering and reports 94% count accuracy and 98% state accuracy on CombatStateBench.

language rules → executable state engine → structured 3D conditioning → renderer

Today’s takeaway

VLM/VLA: Show-Harness suggests that the interface between a general VLM and the robot may be as important as building a larger end-to-end policy.

World models: DUET-DINO and Programmable World Model both make hidden state more explicit—through cross-view latent dynamics in robotics and executable persistent state in interactive worlds.

Geospatial: REMSA points to a new problem created by foundation-model abundance: selecting the right model under modality, task and deployment constraints.
