Sep 27 – Oct 2, 2026

Explicit interfaces move from research representation to agent infrastructure

Weekly synthesis

The research side converged on explicit, reusable intermediate state: geometry tools for metric VLM reasoning, persistent semantic 3D maps, rolling world-action imagination, causal evidence distillation, visual action representations, physical-language world models, persistent observer views and lossless visual memory. Across VLM, VLA and world models, the common move was away from forcing a monolithic model to internally retain every state, action and constraint.

The ecosystem side changed even faster. Jev-style typed probabilistic decisions became an emerging infrastructure category: community routers appeared, Cloudflare launched Jev-compatible Clef models, and AWS Strands released an open retrainable Decider. The strongest cross-week signal is a new separation of concerns: generative models handle rich synthesis/reasoning, while smaller explicit interfaces handle routing, gating, state, evidence and action selection.

Track signals

VLM/LVLM: explicit evidence binding, causal evidence use, query-aware token pruning and persistent visual memory.

VLA/Embodied: reusable skill libraries, visual action interfaces, generated interaction data and language-aligned continuous action fields.

Geospatial AI: global field delineation, satellite-derived navigation, SAR/optical translation and flow-based sensor-domain adaptation.

World Models/Physical AI: rolling imagination, physical-language constraints, persistent observers and explicit 3D control.

Agentic Models: learned tool selection, world-model coaching, decision models and low-latency routing/gating layers.

Generative Modeling: few-step video distillation, counterfactual interaction generation and flow matching increasingly serve action/physical tasks rather than visual quality alone.
